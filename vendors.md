## Vendor & Exhibit Information

Registration provides each vendor with one table. Additional tables or floor space may be added for a cost. Table surface or comparable floor space: 2.5" x 5". If you prefer placement against a wall or with access to electricity, please register early to ensure availability. Tables are provided without covers or drapes. If you wish, you may bring your own. Chairs will be provided.

Set-up is either Thursday starting at 4pm or Friday morning starting at 8am in the Jasper room (#161). If you need to set up earlier than 8am please contact {{ site.data.event.email.vendor_contact_name }} to make arrangements. The vendor hall will officially open after Mass on Friday at approximately 11:30am and close at 8pm.

The vendor hall will reopen on Saturday from 8:30am to 3:00pm when the last talk begins. Please note we do close the vendor hall during our Mass times.

Wifi will be available for those that require it. For more information contact {{ site.data.event.email.vendor_contact_name }} at [{{ site.data.event.email.vendors }}](mailto:{{ site.data.event.email.vendors }}).
